# Sword Showdown — the game in full

> Part of the codebase guide — start at [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).

A browser-based 3D sword-fighting game, **Sword Showdown**. A visitor who isn't signed in starts on the start screen as a guest; the **sea start** (off for now — `startAsGuest({ seaStart: true })` in `authenticate` turns it back on) puts them out on the sea: standing in a canoe with a fishing rod, only 🎣 Rod / 🛶 Oar / ⬅ Lobby / Sign In on screen, up before the island's mappack has loaded; the canoe points at the island's fishing spot (the player faces over its side until 🛶 Oar), and stepping ashore there starts Town as the guest. The player's phone is the sword controller (gyro + joystick over PeerJS, `public/phone-sword.html`); the desktop/TV browser runs the game. Players fight waves of AI swordsmen, bomb throwers and (later stages) wand casters along a path, each stage ending with a boss that talks first, across the island town map (a map-editor `.mappack` with a pirate ship anchored offshore; Classic keeps the static GLB map), stage by stage, collect coins and, in the village between stages, buy upgrades from the market stall (including a magic wand that casts fire / ice blasts for mana), switch characters, and calibrate the sword before each stage. Once the story's final boss (Pemberton) is beaten, Showdown becomes **open roam**: the player walks the island freely, and each story stage's village has that stage's old boss in it with a quest to take back what Pemberton stole — the stage again, with Pemberton as its final enemy; dying or finishing it goes back to roam. In roam, the anchored pirate ship offers **⛵ Explore the Seas**: it sails out to sea and back with the player kept on deck while frog men climb aboard and attack. **Town** is a living town on the island (Brindlewick, the houses in the north-west): one resident to a house — townsfolk and players together (~18 townsfolk + the player; a player joining a shared town takes a house, a townsperson moving out if none is free) — townsfolk with jobs, homes and needs go about their day on a day / night clock; the player can talk to them and give them coins, or hurt them — a crime someone sees brings wanted stars, guards who fight and carry the player to jail (a cell in the jail house — a home interior with bars and the sheriff's desk; bail, bribe, pick the lock or sleep it off), a sheriff with a bow at ★★★; townsfolk hold fading grudges, call family and the brave for help, brawl and mug each other, and friends follow the player; bandits camp far out of town — each night from 21:00, for three town hours, the sheriff looks for the player to help defend the town — accepted, he leads them to a spot on the Endless walk near where he asked and an Endless wave (not the camp's bandits — just there for the wave) comes at them as they auto-walk a stretch of it, with their own Town gear and no auto-buy, a step harder every third night, while every townsperson hides at home (`townDefense.js`); the sheriff fights beside them with his bow (an ally — `townAllies.js`: beside the player a little way out, foes now and then going for him instead, badly hurt he runs home to hide and the foes after him turn back on the player); held off = no raid tonight + a reward; missed, turned down or lost (going down) = the wave's bandits the player didn't beat raid the town (as many of the gang as that); with no sheriff / no weapon the gang (the camp's own bandits: the ones the player beats stay gone, one more turns up each morning, and a bigger gang carries bombs / shields / bows / wands) raids the town as before (breaking into houses, the player's now and then — taking from its chest —, robbing whoever's out, stripping the fields; a raider who runs into the player fights an armed one and holds up an unarmed one — pay, or run and they fight), more of them join as the days go on, and the morning after the player beats some the sheriff comes over with a reward ; with the sword / wand out the camera auto-aims at whoever's coming at the player — at their camp they warn the player off, then fight an armed one (chasing only off their ground) or rob an unarmed one; after the first day the townsfolk's jobs come in **branches** (`BRANCHES` in `town/quests/jobs.js` — the wizard's, the fisher's; each opens once the first day's done or skipped unless its `from` says a day / hours / after another job), each branch's jobs in order (the next only once the one before is done), the branches side by side but one job at a time: givers waiting to come up queue (a giver near the player first) and none comes while another branch's job is under way or the player's in a fight / out on the water / off the island — talking to any waiting giver (❗) starts theirs; the fisher's first job: bring him 10 wood and he builds the canoe (no canoe in Town till then); his second, the golden fish: he kneels in the canoe's bow with the player, glowing arrows on the water lead to a first spot (a plain fish), then further out to the golden fish's spot (no talk boxes out on the water — he just says his lines; at each spot the canoe stops and the rod comes out, the oar back for the next leg), where it bites — and while it's on the line the pirate ship (the map's own, off its anchor — waiting on the horizon) pulls up, a pirate's bomb rocks the canoe and the fish gets away; he paddles slowly off round a ring of open water with the ship chasing and its crew — bomb throwers whose bombs home in to the player's sword, and archers — attacking one at a time, the player (sword out in the canoe) knocks the bombs and parries the arrows back to kill every pirate, the ship sails off and he paddles home (`townPirates.js`, `beats/sea.js`); his third, under the waves: he won't go without the player owning a bow ('come find me once you buy one' — the seek's `needs`), then out in the canoe to where it gets deep, a wizard's potion gives them both an air bubble under water till the job's done (`T.questAir`), both out over the side and down, he swims ahead (`townDiver.js`), shows the player how to spear a fish with the bow on its string, leads them deeper to the golden fish; speared and wound in, they surface to the pirate ship sitting there (moved while the player was under — ship `lurk`): three bombs on them both, black, the player wakes lying in the canoe, the fisher gone (taken — out of the world till a job frees him: `capture` / the quests' `taken` flag), arrows after the fleeing ship till it's away, then back to shore (`beats/dive.js`); his fourth, the rescue: his cousin (another fisher — the cast role `cousin`) asks the player if they've seen him, hears the pirates took him and says they saw the ship off Tree Island's coast, hands over a lock-pick if the player has none, and — by day — says they'll come find the player tonight (a `nightOnly` seek); after dark both cross in the canoe to Tree Island (loaded then — ctx `ensureIsland`), land on its beach facing home (townPirates.js' island plan) and creep along the shore to the ship moored off it (ship `moor`), one pirate on watch at its rail with a lantern: one arrow that kills him and nobody wakes; a miss (or wading too near) and 7 pirates jump down and come at them — then Climb aboard, the fisher in a cage on the deck (bars like the jail's), the lockpick minigame opens it, 7 more pirates come over the rails, back to the canoe (both of them in it — a second seat in the stern) and home, where he gives the player the golden fish (good `goldenFish` — cooked at the campfire it becomes the **golden elixir**: drunk, full health, then 5 minutes of glowing gold and swimming / diving ~2.6× as fast without the swim bar draining) and from then on fights beside them and the sheriff in the night defense (`beats/rescue.js`); the canoe's Get out works anywhere on the water (over the side, swimming) and swimming up to it gets back in; each giver coming to find the player like the first day's guides (a ❗ over them meanwhile — 'Later' and they come back; ❓ for a reward), tracked top right with 📍 markers — day 2: the farmer has the cart brought beside the player loaded with veg for the wizard up the mountain, glowing arrows show the drive up, and the wizard pays at his cabin; then he asks for a ride down to the bandit camp to talk to his son Rowan (who doesn't want to be a wizard) — Rowan's bomb knocks the cart over, he runs off and seven of his bandit friends come at the player as they auto-walk the nearest stretch of the Endless walk (the `wave` beat — the night defense's fight made reusable), the wizard fighting beside the player with his wand (an ally — `townAllies.js`; he goes down → the job restarts), the cart brought round after, then a drive into town — then the bandits' revenge: the wizard's cabin torn down to a heap of dirt and logs (a reusable ruin — `src/environment/ruins.js` + `townRuins.js`), and fifteen logs to chop, load into the cart's bed (the cart holds goods any time — Cart by it) and drive up the mountain; he pays and the cabin's back up by morning; the jobs are quests made of reusable beats (`src/quest/` + `townQuests.js`, data `town/quests/jobs.js`) (skipping the first day hands over a rod and a sword); no other jobs are handed out round town for now; the dead stay dead and newcomers move in; the player's own house is kept for them and bought at its door, with a chest inside for what doesn't fit in the bag (`BAG_MAX` goods) or the wallet (`WALLET_MAX` coins); townsfolk's doors are locked at night (knock: they ask you in, come to the door or don't answer; a job from them or a follower gets you in), other players' homes always, and a bought single-use lock-pick opens any of them (a break-in if seen); in a shared Town (Multiplayer) up to 12 players share one town for good (`townNet.js`): the host's game runs the townsfolk, each player has their own wanted stars, guards chase whoever did it, and others can help — or hunt a wanted player for the bounty. Chickens peck mostly round the farms (a few at a time off on outings anywhere in town / along the roads) and lay eggs; any weapon turns a hen into a drumstick — drumsticks and eggs are picked up, eaten, cooked or sold (at the player's stand or to a farmer) like fish (`townChickens.js`). Its economy runs on wood, fish and veg: each producer sells their own (the fisher fish and fishing rods, the farmer veg and seeds, the woodcutter wood) at a selling spot — one of the two market stalls either side of the shop's door, or one of three spots along each of the shop's side counters — in their selling hours (fish in the morning, veg in the afternoon, so there's always some food), at set prices, and everyone buys food there; the player buys from them (at their spot or by talking to them), sells them their own good back for less, or takes a free spot (Sell here) and, while they stay there, each townsperson who comes up asks to buy something (Sell / No, sorry); the player chops, fishes (with a rod bought from a fisher, held and swung with the gyro like the sword: bubbles rise where the fish are; swing to cast — harder for further, swung across (back-left → out-right / back-right → out-left) to land to that side — and a bobber on the bubbles bites fast with a bigger fish; swipe to hook; the fish swims left / right / out — swipe right / left / up against it; the camera turns once toward where the bobber lands; worn out, the touch reel comes up — the phone's, or the on-screen one without a phone — and cranks it in (the which-way / reel hints only once they've been getting it wrong) — anywhere on foot — the cast goes out in front, landing on water or coming back in (`T.castAhead`)), rides the cart parked by the market (Get in the cart: crouched in its bed, it rolls on the ground where the phone leans — like the broom — anything in hand still usable; Cart: its bed stores up to `CART_MAX` goods from the bag), paddles the canoe moored by the fishing spot once the fisher's built it (standing in it with the oar; the phone's tilt steers it like a joystick: tipped forward it paddles on, back it backs up, leaned left / right it turns that way — the oar's strokes play by themselves to fit) and fishes from it anywhere on the water with the rod, flies the broom bought from the wizard (sitting on it, floating; tilting the phone drifts it that way, it turns toward its drift and the camera swings round behind it) and picks (only their own fields, or a farmer's while helping on the first day), buys the farmers' fields (100 🪙, at the field) to pick from, buys plots of land by the fields (40 🪙 at their signpost — cheaper than a field) and builds up to 3 planter boxes on each (3 wood apiece), plants seeds from the shop only in those boxes (a dirt pile that grows one veg a town day, or in about 40 s once watered with the hose — grabbed at a tap by land the player owns, with empty hands — ✋ Grab / ✋ Drop, like anything grabbable — aimed with the gyro, 💧 Water in place of the Block button), cooks fish, veg, drumsticks and eggs over the campfire (one Cook button → a list like the bag's) into food that heals and sells for more, and trades too, and starts Town with nothing but a few coins (Town's coins, health and gear are its own — Showdown's stay out) and a first day of small guided goals (each one's button ringed like the tutorial's) from the townsfolk — quests made of beats too, like the jobs (`quests/firstDay.js`); townsfolk show how they feel about the player when talked to, remember what the player did (and bring it up), and add up to a town-wide reputation shown in the top bar — even the sword is bought, at the shop (one of the town's houses is the shop — its back counter the weaponsmith's: gear and a mystery chest, shown only while he's in — run by one townsperson, the merchant (role `merchant`, shown as Weaponsmith); its side counters are selling spots, empty unless someone's selling there; the magic wand and the potions are the town wizard's, at his market stall in front of his cabin up the mountain (the Showdown village's stall); walking in through the door puts the player inside the shop room (`shop.glb`), where the things sit on the counters, shopped like the Showdown village's: tap a thing on a counter; townsfolk walk in and out too; every other house is someone's home — walk in through its door into `home_interior.glb`, where its townsfolk go to sleep (seen on the bed), hide, or spend a while; all the rooms sit under the map, out of sight). **Story** is a one-player story mode on Tree Island, saved as it goes (signed in): it opens on a cut scene — black, a lightning flash in the night showing the village boy washed up on a beach of the island's outer coast, black again, then a fade in at sunrise on him sitting on a stone playing a flute, seen from behind — and the **Crow** (a recurring character, `crow.glb`) flies in and asks for the flute: give it (game over, back to the start), throw a rock (a side quest, later) or "What is this place?" — it wants a fish first (the old rod leans in a canoe moored off the beach — the canoe is holed, 3 wood mends it — fish from the shore; bringing the Crow fish is how it gives information and hints); the fish → the Crow tells of a sacred tree inland that can help → the wise tree (`wise_tree.glb`, an old tree with a face in its bark) gives the player a sword → chopping down a tree (Town's tree chopping) and splitting its logs gives wood → the canoe mended → "To be continued…". A frog mom sits on a stone along the way to the sacred tree (no talk yet). The engine (`storyMode.js`) runs a chapter's data (`chapter1.js`), so the story grows by adding data. **Story reuses Town mode's systems as much as possible** (installed on a stand-in for Town's state — see the Story row). **Endless** is Showdown with no end: the player auto-walks every Showdown stage route one after another (starting over on the first after the last) while waves of enemies come at them, each bigger and harder (bomb throwers from wave 2, shield bearers 3, wizards 4, archers 6 — one of a kind the wave it joins, a few more as the waves go on — a champion every 5th wave), until they die (every run starts at level 1 with the base hearts, 0 coins, the sword, the bow (no arrows yet), the magic wand (full mana) and a shield that never breaks; auto-buy still spends what the run earns; the real coins / gear are set aside meanwhile) — the score is the run's kills against their best (kept on the device, so guests and offline play count too, and on the profile when signed in). The game is an installable PWA that plays offline once it has cached itself (everything but Classic's map, Tree Island and the songs). Showdown is single player; **Classic** is a Wii Sports Resort–style take on it (Miis only, swordsmen only, 3 hearts per stage, Block is the only button, minimal HUD). **Multiplayer** mode is a lobby of everyone online where you challenge another player to a 1v1 sword duel in a private room, or start a **Team Battle** (5 v 5) / **Free For All** (10 fighters) with invited players, matchmaking players and bots, each played as **Survival** (last one standing) or **Most Kills** (respawns, most kills when the clock runs out wins); everyone starts with the sword and picks up bows, arrows, shields, bombs, magic wands, mana, hearts, life potions and bubbles scattered around the arena. The old RPG, horde, 3D painter, OSM map and NPC systems were removed. The repo name "qwop-tps" is historical.
